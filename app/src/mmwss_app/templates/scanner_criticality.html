Asset criticality multipliers

Per-site multiplier (0.1 – 5.0, default 1.0) applied to the risk-score formula. Set high-value sites (donation portals, member areas) to 2.0; parked or non-prod to 0.5.

Risk score formula
risk = (CVSS/10 × 50%) + (EPSS × 30%) + (criticality_multiplier/2 × 20%)
Result is clamped to 0–100 and stored on each finding.
